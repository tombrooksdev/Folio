title: Plan a sprint in the backlog
minutes: 3
---
A **sprint** is a short, fixed period (usually one to four weeks) with a goal.

## Plan it

1. Open the **Backlog**. Choose **Create sprint** and give it a name and an optional goal.
2. **Drag issues** from the backlog into the sprint. The sprint header shows how many points you have taken on.
3. Keep an eye on what your team can really finish. The **Reports** page shows your velocity from earlier sprints.
4. Press **Start sprint** and choose how many weeks it lasts.

## Rules that keep things simple

- Only **one sprint** can be active at a time.
- You can still add or remove issues after starting. The burndown chart records the scope change honestly.
- Drag to reorder: the order in a list is the priority order.

> [!TIP]
> Write a goal in one sentence. It appears on the board and keeps everyone pointed the same way.
