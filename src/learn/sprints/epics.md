minutes: 3

Once you have more than a handful of issues, structure helps.

## Epics

An **epic** is a big goal made of many issues, for example *Onboarding redesign*. Create epics from the **Epics** panel at the top of the backlog, then set an issue's **Epic** in its details. The panel shows progress for each epic, and clicking one filters the backlog to it.

## Sub-tasks

A **sub-task** breaks one issue into smaller steps. Open an issue and choose **Add sub-task**. The parent shows how many are done. Sub-tasks go one level deep.

## Links

Choose **Link issue** to connect two issues: *blocks / is blocked by*, *relates to*, or *duplicates / is duplicated by*. Both issues show the link.

## Labels

Labels are free-form tags such as `frontend` or `urgent`. Filter by them on the board and in issue search.

> [!TIP]
> If you are unsure whether something is an epic or a story, ask whether it would take more than one sprint. If so, it is an epic.
