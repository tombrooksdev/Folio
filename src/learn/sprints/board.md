The **Board** is where the sprint happens day to day.

## Move work

**Drag a card** between **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**. Changes save instantly, and everyone watching the issue is told. You can also change status from the issue itself.

## Focus

- **Search this board** filters cards as you type.
- Click an **avatar** to show one person's cards, or press **Only my issues**.
- Filter by **epic** or **label**.
- **Group by** assignee or epic to split the board into swimlanes. Collapse a lane to tuck it away.

## Read a card

A card shows the type icon, the issue key, priority, points and assignee. A small arrow with a key means it is a sub-task of that issue. A done card has its key crossed out.

> [!INFO]
> The progress line at the top shows points done out of points planned, and how many days are left.
