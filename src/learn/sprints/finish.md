## Complete the sprint

When the time is up, press **Complete sprint** on the board. Finished work stays with the sprint. Anything unfinished returns to the backlog, ready for the next one. Then you land on the sprint's report.

## Reports

Open **Reports** in the space:

- **Burndown** compares remaining work with an ideal straight line, and shows any scope added mid-sprint. A line that sits above the ideal means you are behind.
- **Velocity** compares what you committed to with what you completed, sprint by sprint. The average of the last three sprints is a good guide for the next plan.
- **Sprint history** lists every sprint with its result.

Open a sprint's report to see what was completed and what was carried over.

> [!TIP]
> Use the report in your retrospective. Create a *Retrospective* page from the template and link it to the sprint.
