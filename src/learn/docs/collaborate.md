Pages are conversations as much as documents.

## Comments

Scroll to the bottom of a page and add a **comment**. Use **Reply** to answer a specific comment. Comments support Markdown, and you can delete your own.

## Mentions

Type `@` followed by a username, such as `@sam`, in a page, comment or issue. That person gets a notification straight away, even if they weren't watching.

## Issue keys

Writing `ENG-12` links to that issue. It works in pages, comments and descriptions, and is skipped inside code.

## Watching

You **watch** pages you create, edit or comment on. Watching means you hear about later changes and comments. Use **Watch** or **Unwatch** on any page to change it.

> [!INFO]
> You can choose exactly what you hear about in *Notification settings*. See the *Stay in the loop* course.
