---
title: Start from a template
minutes: 2
---
Blank pages are intimidating. **Templates** give you a good outline in one click.

## Use one

Choose **Create page** in the sidebar. The gallery offers:

- **Meeting notes**: agenda, notes, decisions and action items
- **Decision record**: context, options and the outcome
- **Product requirements**: goals, user stories and metrics
- **Retrospective**: what went well, what did not, what to change
- **Runbook**: steps, rollback and contacts
- **How-to guide**: goal, prerequisites and steps

The date and your name are filled in for you. Everything in a template is just text, so change whatever you like.

## Make pages from a page

Open a page and choose **Child page** to start a new page underneath it, for example a meeting note under a project page.

> [!TIP]
> Decision records are worth the habit. Six months from now someone will ask why you chose option B.
