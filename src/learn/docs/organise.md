---
title: Organise with the page tree
minutes: 3
---
A tidy tree is what makes a wiki usable after the first month.

## Parents and children

Every page can sit under another page. Choose **Child page** to create one, or change **Parent** in the editor to **move** an existing page. A page can't be moved under its own children.

The sidebar tree shows the structure. **Drag** a page onto another to rearrange it: drop on the top edge to put it before, the bottom edge to put it after, or the middle to make it a child. (**Move up** and **Move down** at the bottom of the editor do the same without a mouse.) Pages are alphabetical until you order a group by hand. Click the small arrow beside a page to expand or collapse it. Folio remembers what you opened.

## Labels

Add **labels** such as `runbook` or `onboarding` in the editor. Click a label on any page to list every page that shares it.

## Stars and recently viewed

**Star** a page you return to often. Starred pages and the ones you looked at recently appear on your home page.

> [!INFO]
> Aim for three levels at most. If you need more, it is usually a sign to split the space.
