---
title: Version history and compare
minutes: 2
---
Every time you save a page, Folio keeps the previous version. You can't lose work by editing.

## See the history

Open a page and choose **History**. Each saved version is listed with its date and author.

## Compare

Open an old version and choose **Compare with current**. Folio shows a line-by-line difference: green lines were added, red lines were removed, and long unchanged stretches are folded away.

## Restore

If you prefer the older version, choose **Restore this version**. The current text is saved to history first, so a restore can be undone too.

> [!TIP]
> Before a big rewrite, check the history is there, then write fearlessly.
