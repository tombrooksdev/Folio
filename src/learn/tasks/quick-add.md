title: Add a task in one line
minutes: 2
---
The box at the top of every Tasks page understands a few shortcuts. Type them anywhere in the line and Folio shows what it understood before you press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

```
Write the launch post  fri  ~1h30  @sam  !  #Launch
```

| Type | It means |
|---|---|
| `today`, `tomorrow`, `fri`, `next week`, `12 oct`, `+3d` | The due date |
| `~30m`, `~1h30`, `~2h`, `~1d` | How long it will take (a day is 8 hours) |
| `@name` | Give it to someone |
| `!` | Important |
| `#List` | Put it in that list (use a dash for spaces: `#Home-chores`) |
| `*` | Add it to My Day |

Words that do not clearly match stay in the title. `@nobody` or `#bug` is left alone when there is no such person or list.

## Why length matters

My Day and Planned add up the lengths and show the total, such as **3h 15m planned**. When a day goes over your daily capacity (6 hours unless you change it in your account) the total turns amber and says **Over budget**, so you can see overcommitting before it happens.

## Keyboard

<kbd>N</kbd> new task, <kbd>Space</kbd> done, <kbd>D</kbd> due date, <kbd>M</kbd> My Day, <kbd>I</kbd> important, <kbd>Esc</kbd> close the pane.
