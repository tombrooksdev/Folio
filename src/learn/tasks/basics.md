**Tasks** is a quiet to-do list for the small jobs that do not belong in a sprint: "book the venue", "review Sam's page", "send the invoice". Open it from **Tasks** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>T</kbd>.

## Add a task

Type in the box at the top and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. That is all it takes. Click the circle to finish a task, and the star to mark it important.

## The smart lists

| List | What you see |
|---|---|
| **My Day** | What you chose to do today. It starts fresh each morning, and offers your overdue and due-soon tasks as **Suggestions**. |
| **Important** | Tasks you starred. |
| **Planned** | Every task with a date: Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later. |
| **Assigned to me** | Tasks other people gave you. |
| **All tasks** | Everything you can see. |
| **Completed** | The history. Search it. |

## Your own lists

Make lists such as **Launch** or **Admin** from **New list** in the sidebar. Each list has a colour, and you can reorder lists and group them into folders from **List settings**.

## The detail pane

Click a task to open its pane on the right. Add **steps** (a small checklist), a **due date**, a **reminder**, a **repeat**, a **length**, notes, and a link to an issue or a page. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close it.

> [!TIP]
> On any issue or page, **Add to my tasks** turns it into a task that points back to it.
