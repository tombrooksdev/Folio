## Assign a task

Pick **Assigned to** in the pane, or type `@name` when you add the task. The task appears in that person's **Assigned to me** and they are told in their bell. They can add it to *their* My Day and set *their own* reminder without changing yours. When they finish it, you are told who did.

## Share a list

A list is **private** (only you, and not even administrators can read it), **shared** with people you pick, or a **team** list that everyone can see. Open **List settings** on a list to change it. Shared members can **edit** or only **view**. Viewers can see shared and team lists but never change them.

## Repeat and remind

Choose **Repeat** (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, yearly, or every N days, weeks or months). Finishing a repeating task creates the next one on the right date, so a weekday task finished on Friday comes back on Monday. A **reminder** shows in the bell and as a desktop alert if you turned those on, and is only ever sent once.

## Plan the week

The **Week planner** shows seven days. Drag a task onto a day to give it a start time, and its length becomes the height of the block. You can also use **Schedule** on a task if you would rather not drag. **Export dated tasks (.ics)** in the sidebar gives you a calendar file for your calendar app.
