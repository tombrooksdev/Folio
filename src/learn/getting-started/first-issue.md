Issues are units of work: a feature, a bug or a chore.

## Create an issue

Press **C** (or choose **Create, Issue**). Pick the space, give it a **summary**, choose a **type** (story, bug or task), and press **Create**. A message at the bottom confirms it, with a link to open it.

## What goes on an issue

- **Type**: story for something users will notice, bug for something broken, task for everything else.
- **Priority**: high, medium or low.
- **Story points**: a rough size. Points add up so you can see how much work is in a sprint.
- **Assignee**, **epic** and **labels** are optional but make filtering easy.
- **Description** supports Markdown, images and `@mentions`.

Open any issue to edit its details in the panel on the right. Changes save as soon as you leave a field.

> [!INFO]
> Every change is recorded. Open the **History** tab on an issue to see who changed what, and when.

## Try it

Press `C`, create an issue called *Try Folio*, then open it and change its status to *In progress*.
