Folio keeps your team's **documents** and **work** in one place. Every screen has the same few landmarks, so once you know them you know the whole app.

## The top bar

- **Create** (top left) makes a new issue, page or space. Press `C` for a new issue from anywhere.
- **Search** finds pages and issues. Press `/` or `Ctrl K`.
- **Bell** shows what has happened since you last looked.
- **Help** (the `?` button) replays the tour and opens this training area.
- **Palette** switches between eleven themes, light and dark.
- **Your avatar** (far right) opens your account menu: password, notification settings, and sign out.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists your **spaces**. Open one and it expands into **Board**, **Backlog**, **Issues**, **Reports** and **Code**, followed by its page tree. Collapse the whole sidebar with the button beside the logo, or press `[`.

> [!TIP]
> Press `?` at any time to see every keyboard shortcut.

## Try it

Open the **Create** menu and look at the three things you can make. Then close it again with `Esc`. You will use each of them in the next lessons.
