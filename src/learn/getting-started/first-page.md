Pages are where knowledge lives: how-tos, decisions, meeting notes, runbooks.

## Create a page

1. Open a space and choose **Create page** in the sidebar (or **Create, Page**).
2. Pick a **template** or start blank. Templates fill in a sensible outline for you.
3. Give the page a title and write in the left pane. The right pane shows the finished page as you type.
4. Press **Ctrl S** or **Save page**.

## Handy things in the editor

- The toolbar adds bold, italic, headings, lists, quotes, code and links. `Ctrl B` and `Ctrl I` work too.
- **Paste an image** straight into the editor and it is uploaded and embedded.
- Pick a **Parent** to place the page under another one in the tree.
- If your browser crashes, Folio offers to **restore your draft** next time you open the editor.

> [!TIP]
> Keep titles specific. "Deploying the API" is easier to find than "Notes".

## Try it

Create a page called *My first page*, write a heading and a short list, and save it. You will see it appear in the sidebar tree straight away.
