---
title: Choose what you hear about
minutes: 2
---
Open **Notification settings** from the bell menu to choose what reaches you.

## Per type

Switch each type on or off: new pages, new issues, updates to things you watch, comments, assignments, mentions and sprint events. Your choices apply only to you.

## Desktop alerts

Choose **Enable desktop alerts** and allow your browser's permission prompt. While Folio is open in any tab, new notifications also appear as a system alert.

## Team channel (administrators)

An administrator can post new pages, new issues and sprint events to a Slack, Mattermost, Discord or Microsoft Teams channel by setting `FOLIO_WEBHOOK_URL` on the server. Set `FOLIO_PUBLIC_URL` as well so the messages include links.

> [!TIP]
> If the bell feels noisy, turn off *new issues* and *new pages* and keep *mentions* and *assigned to me*. Those two are the ones that need you.
