title: Notifications and watching
minutes: 3
---
Folio tells you when something relevant happens, and stays quiet when it isn't.

## The bell

The **bell** in the top bar shows how many notifications you haven't read. Click it for the latest, press a notification to jump to it (it is marked as read), or choose **Mark all as read**. **View all** opens the full list.

## What you hear about

| When | You are told if |
|---|---|
| A new page is created | You are in the team (everyone) |
| A new issue is created | You are in the team (everyone) |
| An issue you watch changes | You are watching it |
| Someone comments | You are watching the page or issue |
| An issue is assigned to you | Always |
| You are mentioned with `@name` | Always |
| A sprint starts or ends | You are in the team |

## Watching

You automatically **watch** what you create, are assigned, edit or comment on. Press **Watch** or **Unwatch** on any page or issue to change that.

> [!INFO]
> You are never notified about your own actions.
